## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-24

First release: the harness, its validation and the quick study.

### Added

- Canonical LP model (`lpmodel.py`): extraction from HiGHS (free rows dropped), seeded row
  and column permutations, exact free-format MPS writer, npz storage.
- Independent verifier (`verify.py`): relative primal and dual residuals, duality gap and
  an evaluation floor, reported at 1e-4, 1e-6 and 1e-8, plus objective agreement with a
  reference value.
- Instance pipeline: independent expander for Netlib's compressed MPS format (checksums
  validated on all 92 files), Koch (2004) exact optima, MIPLIB 2017 benchmark manifest,
  `fetch` (allow-listed hosts, timeouts, size cap, SHA-256 checksums) and `prepare`.
- Adapters for seven configurations: `highs-dsimplex`, `highs-ipx`, `highs-pdlp`,
  `highs-hipdlp`, `ortools-glop`, `ortools-pdlp`, `scip-soplex`, with a per-epsilon option
  table copied verbatim into reports.
- Runner: persistent single-thread workers, hard deadlines with kill and respawn, atomic
  per-unit records, resume on rerun, progress log, `plan` with a pilot-based projection,
  and `scripts/run_study.py` (retry wrapper with path-scrubbed log).
- `lpbench reference`: consensus objectives for 112 of 114 MIPLIB relaxations from three
  simplex codes at 1e-9.
- Statistics (`stats.py`): shifted geometric means, PAR-k, percentile, BCa and two-level
  bootstrap, performance profiles with pointwise and simultaneous bands, Wilcoxon with
  Holm correction, rank probabilities, Kendall tau, variability measures.
- `lpbench report`: outcome, verification, SGM, pairwise, profile, tolerance, variability,
  noise and ranking-sensitivity tables, regenerated byte-identically from the same records.
- Quick study results (`docs/results/quick/`, `REPORT.md`) and the pilot config.
- Minimal reproduction of HiGHS PDLP stopping early with a finite time limit
  (`docs/repros/`).

### Changed

- SCIP runs with symmetry handling off (it added constraints to pure LPs), and
  `highs-pdlp`'s time limit is enforced by the runner (DESIGN 12, items 12 and 14).

### Scaffold (0.0.1)

- uv project with exactly pinned solver versions (highspy 1.15.1, ortools 9.15.6755,
  pyscipopt 6.3.0) and `uv.lock`, CI (pull request and manual dispatch), hygiene files,
  `docs/DESIGN.md`, `lpbench versions`, environment tests.
